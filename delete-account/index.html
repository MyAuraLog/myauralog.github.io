Delete your MyAura Log account

Last updated: October 7, 2026

You can delete your MyAura Log account and all the data linked to it at any time, directly in the app or by email.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open MyAura Log and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Edit Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted immediately.

Request deletion without the app

Send an email to auralog2525@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject "Account deletion – MyAura Log". We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, user ID, name and profile photo)
  • All your migraine logs, including symptoms, triggers, medications and notes
  • Your daily check-ins (water intake and stress level)
  • Your reminder settings and notification tokens

Deleted data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered. Purchase records are kept by Google Play or Apple under their own policies; we do not keep any.

Deleting your account does not cancel your Premium subscription. Cancel it first in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings.

Delete some data but keep your account

To delete a single migraine entry, go to History in the app, open the entry and tap the red bin icon.

To delete more of your data (for example your entire migraine history or your stress check-ins) while keeping your account, email auralog2525@gmail.com from the address linked to your account with the subject "Data deletion request – MyAura Log". We will process your request within 30 days.

For more details, see our Privacy Policy.
