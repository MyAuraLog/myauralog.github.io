Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyAura Log ("the app", "we") collects, uses, stores and protects your information. MyAura Log is published by Emmanuel Dubois, an individual based in Switzerland (the "data controller"). Android package ID: com.mycompany.auradesignsystem.

This policy applies together with our Terms of Service.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a unique user ID. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and, where provided, your name and profile photo from that service. Your profile photo is only used to display your account in the app. We never receive your Google or Apple password.

Health information you enter. MyAura Log is a migraine diary. We store the information you choose to log, including migraine episodes (start and end times, duration, intensity, symptoms, possible triggers, medications taken and related notes) and your daily stress level. This is health data and is treated as sensitive personal data.

Hydration data. We store your reminder preferences (for example the reminder interval) and the water intake you log.

Device information for notifications. To send reminders and notifications (such as hydration reminders and automatic closing of ongoing migraine episodes), we store a push notification token linked to your device via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Purchase information. If you subscribe to Aura Premium, we receive your subscription status and purchase history (plan, purchase date, trial and renewal status) through RevenueCat. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card or payment details.

Advertising data. In the free version, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions, to serve and measure ads.

2. How we use your information

We use your information only to provide the app's features: storing and displaying your migraine and stress history, generating your reports, sending the reminders and notifications you enable, managing your account and Premium subscription, and, in the free version, displaying ads. We do not sell your personal data. Your health data is never used for advertising and is never shared with advertisers.

3. Legal basis

We process your health data on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you create an account and choose to log this information. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your data or your account (see section 8). Other data is processed to provide the service you requested (account, reminders, subscription) and, for personalized advertising, on the basis of your consent collected through the consent form shown in the app.

4. PDF reports

The app lets you generate a PDF report of your migraine and stress history, for example to share with your doctor. The report is generated on your device and shared only through the method you choose (email, messaging app, etc.). We do not send it to anyone on your behalf.

5. Service providers

We rely on the following providers, which process data on our behalf. You can read their own privacy policies for more details:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, cloud functions, push notifications), operated by Google LLC – Firebase privacy information
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) – RevenueCat privacy policy
  • Google AdMob (advertising, free version only) – How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store (payments) – Google privacy policy, Apple privacy policy

These providers may process data on servers outside Switzerland and the European Union, notably in the United States, under safeguards required by applicable law, such as standard contractual clauses.

6. Advertising

The free version of MyAura Log displays banner ads provided by Google AdMob on some screens (such as the Dashboard, History and Settings). The app does not show full-screen or video ads.

Your health data is never used for advertising. Ads are not based on your migraine logs, symptoms, medications, stress level or water intake, and none of this information is shared with Google AdMob or any advertiser.

Consent. Where required by law (for example in the EU, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can refuse and will then see non-personalized ads. On iOS, your device's advertising identifier is only accessed if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can also reset or limit the use of your advertising ID in your device settings.

Ad-free with Aura Premium. Subscribing to Aura Premium removes all ads from the app for as long as your subscription is active.

7. Data security and retention

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored on Google Firebase's secured infrastructure. Access to your data is restricted to your account. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account from the app, your personal data, including all your health data, is permanently deleted immediately; requests sent by email are processed within 30 days. Purchase records held by Google Play or Apple are kept by them under their own policies.

8. Deleting your account and data

In the app: go to Settings, Edit Profile, tap Delete account and confirm. Your account and all associated data (migraine logs, daily check-ins, reminder settings, notification tokens) are permanently deleted immediately.

Without the app: send an email to auralog2525@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject "Account deletion – MyAura Log". We will process your request within 30 days.

Deleting specific data without deleting your account: you can delete individual migraine entries directly in the app: go to History, open the migraine entry you want to delete, and tap the red bin icon. You can also ask us to delete some or all of your data (for example your entire migraine history or your stress check-ins) while keeping your account, by emailing auralog2525@gmail.com from the address linked to your account with the subject "Data deletion request – MyAura Log". We will process your request within 30 days. Deleted data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Please cancel it in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings.

A step-by-step guide is also available on our account deletion page.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and the EU GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

10. Children

MyAura Log is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. Medical disclaimer

MyAura Log is a personal tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be communicated in the app.
